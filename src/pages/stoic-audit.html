The STOIC Audit is a 5-day operational audit for UK property management and lettings firms. It costs £997, one-off. We look at how the operation actually runs, trace where time and money go, and rank what we find. Book a free Snapshot Call to start.

What the audit covers

STOIC stands for Survey, Trace, Order, Interrogate and Cost. Each step builds on the one before it.

  • Survey. We look at the operation as it actually works, and hold leadership’s view up against what happens on the ground.
  • Trace. We follow one job through every department to see where the time and money actually go.
  • Order. We rank what we find by impact against effort and keep the vital few. What we leave alone gets named.
  • Interrogate. We pressure-test the opportunities on an impact and effort matrix, and go looking for hidden dependencies before anything gets bought. You get the top three, in order.
  • Cost. We put a number on it, in time and money. Hours reclaimed × rate × 52 = annual value.

What you get

A costed list, not an opinion: the problem, with a number on it, and the top three opportunities in order. You can see how the sum works on your own figures with the savings calculator on the homepage.
