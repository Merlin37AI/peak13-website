An example of how a build works

A maintenance request at a lettings agency, in five steps. This is an illustration of how a build works, not a client case.

  1. Trigger. A tenant emails “The boiler has stopped again.”
  2. It reads the message. Finds the property, the tenancy and how urgent it is.
  3. It checks the rules. Contractor rota, landlord spend limit, repair history.
  4. It does the job. Books the contractor, tells the tenant, logs the work.
  5. A person steps in. Anything unclear or over the limit goes to your team, with the full picture attached.
