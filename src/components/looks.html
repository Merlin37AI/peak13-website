What the auditlooks for.

Waste is rarely one big thing. It sits in small repeated jobs that nobody has written down.

  • Work done twiceThe same details typed into more than one system, sheet or inbox.
  • Handoffs that waitJobs that sit until a person, an inbox or a reply moves them on.
  • Chasing by handReminders, status checks and follow-ups that someone has to remember.
  • ReworkErrors and missed details that send a job round the loop again.
  • One-person jobsTasks that stall when a particular person is away.
