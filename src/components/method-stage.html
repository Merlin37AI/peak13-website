  1. Step 1: The whole building

    Survey

    01 / 05

    From the street it looks like one solid building. Go floor by floor and you see how it really runs. We look at the operation as it actually works, and hold leadership's view up against what happens on the ground.

  2. Step 2: The track

    Trace

    02 / 05

    We follow one job through every department to see where the time and money actually go. Green means the work flows. Red marks the waste, where it happens.

  3. Step 3: The vital few

    Order

    03 / 05

    Most of what we find isn't worth chasing yet. We rank it by impact against effort and keep the vital few. What we leave alone gets named, and stays on the drawing as an outline.

  4. Step 4: The matrix

    Interrogate

    04 / 05

    Then we pressure-test the opportunities on an impact and effort matrix. A fix that looks simple on a whiteboard usually hides a dependency nobody mentioned, so we go looking for it before anything gets bought. You get the top three, in order.

  5. Step 5: Loss and recovery

    Cost

    05 / 05

    Last, we put a number on it, in the two measures that matter: time and money. What we find is the loss. What we build is the recovery. Whatever one week gives back, the year gives back 52 times.

    Hours reclaimed × rate × 52 = annual value
